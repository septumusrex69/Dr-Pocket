DR POCKET LOGO, VECTOR MASTER (16 Nov 2026)

Rebuilt from approved-symbol-extracted-transparent.png and approved-logo-extracted-transparent.png
(the low-resolution originals). Not auto-traced: the outline was measured from the original and
rebuilt from clean geometry. The arms all lean at the same angle, the corners are true curves, the
S-shaped gap is a smooth curve, and the shape turns 180 degrees onto itself (as the original nearly
does). It matches the original outline to about 95% (the rest is the blur around the original's
edges). The gaps between the petals are transparent, never white.

Colours (from the brand guide): Deep Navy #0B2342, Petrol Teal #006F7F, Turquoise #00C7D9,
Medical Red #EF2B3F. Each petal has its own gradient, fitted to the shading of the original, with
a soft shadow where the upright arm folds over the side arms.
Wordmark: Montserrat Bold Italic converted to outlines (no font needed), tracking -32, "Dr" #0B2342,
"Pocket" #17465E (both sampled from the original). The guide names SemiBold Italic; Bold matches the
approved image much more closely. Swap the weight if you prefer, but use outlines.
