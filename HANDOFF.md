## What the app does

1. **3D body** built from BodyParts3D, with one compressed model per body system in `public/models/`.
   - Turn, zoom to the cursor, and double-tap to zoom into a spot.
   - Tap a part to name it.
   - Search parts and whole organs.
   - Show, hide or see through each body system.
   - One-tap views: Skin, Muscles, Organs, Skeleton, Nerves, Vessels.
   - Region buttons: Whole body, Head, Chest, Hips and more.
2. **Man / Woman** switch.
   - The woman is the male model reshaped in code: face, brow, waist, hips, breasts and procedural long hair.
   - Male-only parts are hidden.
   - Drawn female organs: uterus, cervix, vagina, ovaries, tubes, breasts and urethra.
   - A drawn vulva replaces the male groin skin.
3. **Explain my scan report.**
   - You paste text, take photos or upload a PDF (up to 8 pages).
   - Claude returns structured JSON: findings mapped to real model parts, plain-language explanations, quotes from the report, unclear parts, background, a glossary and questions for the doctor.
   - You can ask follow-up questions.
4. **Narrated walkthrough** ("short film").
   - The camera director establishes the whole view, pushes in and orbits each finding.
   - Each finding gets a pulsing pin and film-style subtitles.
   - The voice is the browser's built-in speech.
   - Structures the model lacks are drawn on the fly, like a labrum or a meniscus.
5. **My reports:** an archive kept in IndexedDB on the device, holding pages, explanations and follow-ups. It has backup and restore. Nothing is stored on the server.
6. **Shoulder walkthrough** (`/shoulder/`): ported from the Shoulder Lab artifact. It shows arm movement and cases (`?case=healthy|bankart|slap|cuff|oa|tight`).

## Conventions and gotchas

- **Coordinates:** Y-up metres; the front is +z; the body's right side is −x.
- **Rendering:** the viewer renders on demand (a dirty flag). It uses quick frames while moving (no AO, lower pixel ratio) and full quality when still. Call the existing redraw helpers after any change, or nothing appears.
- **Groin:** the male groin skin is cut away in a shader (`clipSkin`, uniform `uFemale`), and the drawn vulva surface covers the hole. The V-shaped cut-out (`vShape`) and the shader's limits must stay in step.
- **Splines:** `curve()` is a Catmull-Rom spline. The earlier eased segments caused visible terraces.
- **Drawn parts:** the drawn surfaces use `depthWrite`, which keeps AO from banding them.
- **Model updates:** changing a model means re-running the pipeline and committing `public/models/`. The URL version updates by itself.
- **BodyParts3D quirks:**
  - The "Central canal" only covers the neck, so the spinal cord is traced through the canal by `build_added.py`.
  - Some parts were misclassified. Fixed rules in `classify.py` cover the bladder vs gallbladder mix-up and oral vs temporal.
- **Testing:**
  - Run Playwright with swiftshader against `npx vite preview --port 4173 --strictPort`, with `/api` routes mocked.
  - The test scripts live in the old session's scratchpad and are not in the repo.
  - To check that a deploy is live, look at the Vercel deployments.
